# Third-party code

- `core/` contains adapted LGM components from [3DTopia/LGM](https://github.com/3DTopia/LGM), based on revision `fe8d12cff8c827df7bb77a3c8e8b37408cb6fe4c`. Its MIT notice is retained in the root `LICENSE`. The model, loader, local LPIPS weight loading, and camera schedule have been adapted for this approximate implementation.
- `core/attention.py` retains the Meta copyright header from [DINOv2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2). The Apache 2.0 license is included in `licenses/DINOv2-LICENSE`.
- `core/lpips.py` adapts the VGG perceptual-loss structure from [PerceptualSimilarity](https://github.com/richzhang/PerceptualSimilarity), loading weights from local files. Its upstream notice is included in `licenses/LPIPS-LICENSE`.
- `scripts/install_rasterizer.sh` fetches [ashawkey/diff-gaussian-rasterization](https://github.com/ashawkey/diff-gaussian-rasterization) at a pinned revision. It is an external dependency with its own license; its source and binaries are not vendored here.
- The optional renderer uses pyrender and trimesh as installed dependencies. The THuman scans, rendered data, and model weights are not redistributed.
- `assets/results/ours_renderpeople_*.gif` shows selected model reconstructions of [Renderpeople free samples](https://renderpeople.com/free-3d-people/), using the existing adapted checkpoint for inference only. Original meshes and textures are not redistributed. Sources, candidate review metrics, and rendering details are recorded in `assets/results/provenance_renderpeople.json`.
- `assets/results/ours_humman_*.gif` shows selected OURS-only reconstructions from four views rendered from HuMMan-Recon scans, using the existing checkpoint without further training. Candidates include 18 provider test-split subjects and eight train-split subjects, all used only for zero-shot inference. These are curated scan-rendered examples, not evaluations on captured Kinect images or a representative benchmark. Sources, selection, rigid alignment, UV-safe cleanup, and rendering details are recorded in `assets/results/provenance_humman.json`. HuMMan is distributed under the S-Lab License 1.0 for non-commercial use; the notice is retained in `licenses/HuMMan-LICENSE`. Please cite Cai et al., *HuMMan: Multi-Modal 4D Human Dataset for Versatile Sensing and Modeling*, ECCV 2022 ([official dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/caizhongang/HuMMan)). Original meshes and textures are not redistributed here.
